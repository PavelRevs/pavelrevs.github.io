Quaqua

A private email outreach tool

Quaqua is a small desktop application that its owner runs on their own computer to send and follow up business emails from their own mailboxes, and to keep track of replies and related tasks.

It is not a public service: there is no sign-up and no hosted version. Only the owner and the people they invite use it, each with their own mailbox.
